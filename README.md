# 📊 Sales Performance Dashboard (Data Analyst Project)

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Sales Performance Dashboard** is an end-to-end **Data Analyst portfolio project** designed to demonstrate practical skills in **data cleaning, exploratory data analysis (EDA), business analytics, and dashboarding** using **Python, Excel, and Power BI**.

The project focuses on converting raw sales data into **actionable business insights**. A major highlight is the application of **Pareto Analysis**, which reveals that the **top 20% of products generate nearly 80% of total sales**, enabling data-driven prioritization of high-impact products.

---

## 🧩 Dataset Description

The dataset is based on a **Superstore Sales dataset** containing ~10,000 transaction records with:

* Order and shipping details
* Customer segmentation and regional data
* Product hierarchy (Category → Sub-Category → Product)
* Sales, profit, discount, and quantity metrics
* Time-based features (Year, Month)
* Derived KPIs (Profit Margin)

This structure closely resembles real-world retail sales data used in business analytics.

## 🔄 Analytical Workflow

### 1️⃣ Data Cleaning & Preparation (Python)

* Standardized data types for dates, numeric, and categorical columns
* Normalized sales and profits and created two features **sales_normalized** and **profit_normalized**
* Created derived fields such as **Year**, **Month**, and **Profit Margin**
* Automated preprocessing to ensure repeatable analysis

### 2️⃣ Exploratory Data Analysis (Excel)

* Analyzed sales and profit by:

  * Region
  * Category and Sub-Category
  * Month
* Used Pivot Tables for quick aggregation and validation
* Conducted **Pareto Analysis** by:

  * Ranking products by sales
  * Calculating cumulative sales percentage
  * Segmenting products into **P1–P5 (20% buckets)**

### 3️⃣ Dashboard Development (Power BI)

* Analyzed sales by:

  * Region
  * Category and Sub-Category
  * Year and Month
  * Product
  * Segment
    
Built an interactive dashboard including:

* **KPI Cards**: Total Sales, Total Profit, Total Quantity
* **Bar Charts**: Sales by Region, Category,Sub-category, Year, Month, Segment and product 
* **Pie Chart**: Product contribution (P1–P5 Pareto segmentation)
* **Slicers**: Region
* **Map**: Map of regions of America analized

## 📈 Key Business Insights

* 📌 **Top 20% of products contribute ~80% of total sales**, validating Pareto principle
* 📌 Certain regions consistently outperform others in revenue and profitability
* 📌 Seasonal trends highlight peak sales periods for better demand forecasting
